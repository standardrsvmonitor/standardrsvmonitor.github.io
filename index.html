Fee income

i
Token trading fees are known per three-day epoch and are allocated evenly across its days; the current epoch is estimated from ETH held in the tax hook and fee splitter. Charter proceeds are recorded on the sale date. Pool fee income is derived from the fee field of each swap, weighted by the reserve’s share of in-range liquidity.

Token trading fees, charter auction proceeds and pool fee income, in USD.
Source: SR indexer epoch-routed events, charter purchase events, PoolManager swap logs, Coinbase ETH-USD.
i
In expansion epochs 70% is allocated to the reserve; in contraction epochs 70% is allocated to buybacks and burns. A further 15% purchases STANDARD into the Incentives Vault and 15% is allocated to the team. Gold denotes charter auction proceeds within the total.

ETH routed by the fee engine at each epoch settlement.

Auctions

i
One charter is offered per 12-hour period in a Dutch auction. The price opens at three times the previous clearing price and halves every two hours toward a floor of 1 ETH until a purchase occurs. Proceeds are distributed through the fee engine. Triangles denote purchases executed through a contract.
Charter auction clearing prices
Clearing price of each charter sold, in ETH.
Source: CharterPurchased events on the auction contract; current price from the auction contract.
i
Expansion licenses open additional branches, are paid in STANDARD and are burned on purchase. Twenty-five are offered per 12-hour period (24-hour periods at launch), opening at twice the previous clearing price with a floor of approximately two days of one branch’s issuance.
License auction clearing prices and volume
Period close price in STANDARD and licenses sold per period.
Source: licenses-purchased events grouped by auction period.

Supply and market capitalization

i
Outstanding supply equals minted supply less STANDARD held in the protocol’s pool less the Incentives Vault. Free float further excludes S-Bills locked and the team wallet; fully diluted adds the unminted ledger balance owed to bankers. DexScreener and GeckoTerminal quote market capitalization on minted supply. The ledger balance has been credited to branches but is minted only on withdrawal.

Supply changes when withdrawals are minted (+), deposits are burned to branches (−) and buybacks are burned (−). S-Bill premiums are paid from a pot held by the S-Bills contract and move tokens within outstanding supply.

Banking system

i
Ledger balance equals issuance plus deposits plus recycled fees, less license purchases, gross withdrawals and revocations. The resolution fee, the exit fee on withdrawals, is 2% and rises to 60% as seven-day exit pressure approaches 10% of the ledger.

Daily deposits against gross withdrawals, with bankers’ ledger balance, which is minted only on withdrawal.
Source: deposited and withdrawn events. Ledger = issuance + deposits + recycled fees − licenses − gross withdrawals − revocations.
